# Cells and DGGS, trajectories, urban and landscape, and recipes (2026-11-19)

- Scope: 24 directories of `modules/analysis/src/gpu-spatial-analysis` (about 67,000 lines) plus `modules/analysis/src/geospatial-analysis`, with their specs and `docs/api` pages.
- All 16 findings of the 2026-11-17 review in this area (DGGS-01 to 04, TRJ-01 to 04, RCP-01 to 05, LAND-01, URB-01) are fixed or recorded as a documented change. Fifteen are FIXED with a pinning test; RCP-01 is PARTLY (the recipe now describes the permutation family honestly, but the two families still differ).
- 36 new findings: 11 wrong-result (`W`), 5 visible-failure (`S`), 13 contract or documentation (`C`), 3 test-gap (`T`) and 4 maintainability (`M`) rows. Each `W` row traces to source or a CPU transcription; none was run on a GPU. Four rows are leads marked UNVERIFIED (DGGS-107, DGGS-110, DGGS-111, URB-101).
- The pattern that survived the fix rounds is optional completeness. Four trajectory contributors (thinning, staypoints, zone events, mobility entropy) return a plausible wrong value when a search or sequence budget is hit, and say so only through a status view the caller may omit (TRJ-102, TRJ-103, TRJ-105).
- One consumer still reads the old meaning of `events.status.overflow`: the showcase satellite scene hides candidate-scratch loss (TRJ-104). The library contributors and recipes were updated correctly.
- The LR-10 claim that every `add...Recipe` function is atomic is tested for the recipe barrels and not for the 13 public urban and landscape recipes, one of which fails it. `addSegregationDecompositionRecipe` leaves 61 `graph.add` calls on the caller graph after a rejected `randomization.replicates` (RCP-101, executed).
- Two cell-index wrong results need small fixes: the HEALPix belt wrap in f32 (DGGS-104) and the H3 outline neighbour rule near the poles (DGGS-101). The CPU twins of point-to-cell agree with h3-js, s2-geometry, a geohash closed form and the healpix-geo fixture.
- Trajectory time handling, track boundaries and the reworked Brownian estimator held up under CPU probes and source reads of the kernels that were opened. Trajectory encounters, the zone-event and restriction kernel bodies and the GPU Kalman kernels were not read.
