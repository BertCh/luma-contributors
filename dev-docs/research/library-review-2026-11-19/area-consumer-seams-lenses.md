## Limits of this report

The cross-family ledgers cover the identified semantic migrations and their traced callers. The public-declaration sweep is broader than the semantic audit. It does **not** establish an exhaustive per-site verdict for every changed overload, inherited field, numeric word index, dynamic string key, generated snippet or cast across all four consumer roots. In particular, manual reprojection layouts, all packed-hull/isovist paths, every trajectory scene, and every website illustration were not verified individually.

Kepler coverage is 40/229 bindings, selected across families and engines. The remaining 189 were inventoried, not read end to end. No complete class-by-class documentation/catalog/kernel-audit census, assertion-free-spec census or NaN-comparator census was established. The independent-oracle count of 26 is a lower bound. The archive evidence applies to saved outputs on one recorded adapter family. Source-only review cannot establish behavior on a second backend, live submission ordering, or the GPU outcomes of SEAM-108 and SEAM-109.

The November 18 gates are historical evidence on the landed tree. This review adds reproducible host counterexamples, source traces and named GPU discriminators; it does not certify the concurrently changing checkout for release.
