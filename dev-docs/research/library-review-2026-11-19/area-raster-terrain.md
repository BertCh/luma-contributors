# Raster and terrain (2026-11-19)

The working tree fixes 13 of the 15 November 17 findings. RT-09 and RT-14 remain partly open. The geographic zonal default and terrain shear guards changed during this review. Both repairs are present in the inspected working tree and absent from the inspected commit.  
Three failures can be reproduced without a GPU through public numerical APIs. Changing units changes an EOF decomposition’s explained variance, a rank-deficient PCA produces parameters its projection contributor rejects, and streamline integration emits an endpoint outside its velocity field.  
Two further defects are visible in graph construction. A filled-elevation request can leave both requested flat-status words unwritten. Fractional embedding zone means have no binding for the producer’s completeness information.  
The highest-priority device probes are a two-cell D8 cycle, a one-iteration skeleton, an exact observation beside a long temporal gap, and whole-versus-window relief with a distant elevation anchor. This review records predicted device outputs separately from observed ones.  
The cost-distance emulator produces the same final costs in 112, 123 and 173 rounds on the continuation spec’s own spiral. The device spec still requires independently scheduled runs to report the same count.  
There are 31 active findings or leads in the table below: 10 W, 4 S, 11 C, 3 T and 3 M. Reach is 14 ORDINARY, 16 UNUSUAL and 1 EXTREME. These counts include explicitly marked shader leads and exclude closed findings and documented limits.

### Per-class no-data and chunk contracts

The 71 classes below are the complement of band consumers. A packed direction, date, state, geometry or seed-code input has a different contract from a scalar value band. Published scalar bands normally check raw nodata and zero validity before scale/offset. Their invalid canonical value is NaN. A caller-owned validity word greater than one normally means usable. The following exceptions matter when wiring outputs.

|---|---|

Both top stream words are unresolved. Watersheds ignores invalid pour points, picks the lowest pour-point **row** on duplicates, otherwise labels by terminal cell index; malformed/outward directions can terminate. StreamOrder treats `ffffffff` as unknown and `fffffffe` as membership. StreamNetwork reserves both top words and publishes a bounded prefix of cell-index edges. Most terrain, geometry, region, field and neighborhood operations take packed GPU views. For time series, most pixel-wise contributors accept packed or cell-chunked layouts through the topology helpers; snapshot covariance is packed-only. A chunk of pixels still needs the required time history per pixel. Seven embedding contributors accept pixel chunks; their training vectors, coefficients or query lists can remain packed. Chunked input, spatial halo tiling and host paging are three different capabilities.

Named `output.status` is common in iterative terrain and composition. Exceptions include depression hierarchy’s required packed `[stored,required,overflow]` plus optional named copies, branching flow’s per-cell status, breach’s `pitStatus`, fractional zonal distribution’s per-zone bit mask, packed registration/remapping/unmixing and k-means diagnostics, and harmonic/BFAST/COLD cell summaries. In the 20-class composition slice, seven have named status objects. In the 32-class temporal/filter slice, 15 have them. Do not count a generic `status` declaration as proof that an incomplete result can reach its next consumer safely.

## Maintainability

**Locality and tile ownership.** The following dependencies were derived from sampler/stencil reads and checked against declared stages. A bound on source reads is only one part of whole-versus-tile equality. Common coordinates, phase, output ownership, global normalization and numerical representation can each affect the core.

| Operators | Required support and evidence for equality |
|---|---|
| Raster focal pairs, rank filter, GLCM texture | Square radius `r` for paired samples and rank, both capped at 8 for the audited 17×17 implementations. Texture needs its window radius because both endpoints must be inside the window. Focal-pair halo tests and `r/remote-sensing/round-two.spec.ts:67` compare cores and pair counts. Rank’s whole/window and tie-boundary assertions are weaker. |
| Fill nodata, sampling/profile, reprojection | Fill reads original donors within `maximumDistance` (≤256); it documents manual halo tiling. Sampling bilinear needs the positive-weight 2×2 taps; bicubic uses `floor(u)−1…floor(u)+2` and can fall back to bilinear. Reprojection needs transformed source support and the real border policy. No constant destination radius discovers the required nonlinear source window. |
| Derivatives, curvature, terrain flow field, relief shading | Interior first derivatives use one cell; one-sided extrapolation needs two inward cells. Curvature needs one or two depending on method and the largest requested ring; diagonal ring displacement is `round(r/√2)`. Terrain flow field needs one cell; relief shading uses one or two plus separately provided pointwise layers. `t/terrain-halo-one-sided.spec.ts:140` distinguishes the real west boundary from a cropped one. |
| TPI, DEV, DEVmax, Weiss, ruggedness | Largest outer radius for TPI family. Vector ruggedness needs `maximumRadius+1` for normals plus averaging. |
| Local relief, multiscale relief, local dominance, relief blend | Simple relief’s geometric support is its radius. Multiscale uses the largest uniform radius or largest row-dependent ground half-width; RT-128 can invalidate numerical equality on SAT paths. Dominance uses the maximum baked row/column offset and edge clamping. Blend is pointwise with shared stretch parameters; independently estimated percentiles are global. |
| Critical points, scalar ridges, summits, peak snap, profile peaks | Critical/ridge classification needs the 3×3 ring. Summits and peak snap have bounded searches, with extra bilinear support for candidate heights in snap; there is no general advertised tile stage. Greedy profile suppression can have an arbitrarily long dependence chain, even with finite local prominence windows. |
| Terrain contours, isolines, isobands and rings | One forward marching-cell corner in each axis; independent runs also need half-open cell ownership, shared edge IDs and placement. | Horizon, cast shadow, point visibility, geomorphons | Horizon march needs `R+1` for bilinear corners. | Texture shading, decode and repair | Texture shading sums scaled blur radii and reconstruction support; decimated levels also need expanded-origin phase, RT-136. RGB decoding is pointwise. Spike repair depends on connected components, the largest component and seam votes. |

CLAHE uses up to four contextual histograms, requiring their actual tile contents and shared interpolation phase. The spectral filter uses a whole-domain periodic FFT; a missing input can invalidate the field. |
| Advection and hydrology | Field integration follows data-dependent paths and needs all interpolation taps until termination. Tiled hydrology separates halo rows, owned runoff, boundary forest and replay. Halo cells contribute no second runoff injection; state and output there reset. ## Limits of this report

The CPU results, validation errors and graph bindings above were observed. No shader output was observed in this review. Device-specific contraction, denormal handling, dispatch ordering and adapter limits remain material to the marked leads. Reading an existing reference assertion establishes its discriminator, not a pass on this working tree.

Hydrology coverage did not exhaust all 15,646 lines, all 65 slice specs, generic merge-tree internals or every operation lowering. Visibility coverage was shallower in planner permutations, every solar-frame combination and fixture payloads. Moving-field coverage did not independently prove complete prominence-tree or ring assembly. Temporal coverage did not exhaust all 63 files or emitted specializations. Embedding/remote coverage sampled registration, patch ground weighting and upstream search internals. Composition coverage sampled whole-cell zonal, algebra/profile dispatches, fractional distribution and wrappers. The report therefore does not assign a clean bill of health to every shader, comparator, output alias or exception path.

The committed census, historical gate record and later working-tree repairs describe different snapshots. The most valuable remaining verification is the small device-probe set above on the exact tree intended for publication.
