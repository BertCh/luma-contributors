# Geometry, joins and search (2026-11-19)

- The 2026-11-17 geometry findings GEO-01 to GEO-12 and ERG-02 held: 10 are FIXED with a test that fails if the fix is undone, GEO-04 is a documented LIMIT (documentation only, behaviour unchanged) and GEO-12 is PARTLY done (multipart feature joins for three predicates).
- The fixes left their classes alive elsewhere. The unscaled circumcentre formula (GEO-05) survives in `GPUMinimumBounds` (GEO-106). The unwrapped-longitude problem (GEO-04) survives in geographic group bounds (GEO-104). The candidate-loss flag (GEO-03) is still optional at the producers (GEO-103, GEO-112).
- 9 wrong-result findings (W) were found, 1 visible failure (S), 21 contract findings (C), 1 grouped test finding (T) and 1 grouped maintainability finding (M). None of the W findings is ORDINARY reach except GEO-102, and that one is in the CPU twin only. Seven were reproduced on the CPU or by a scalar port of the kernel arithmetic; GEO-103, GEO-107, GEO-108 and GEO-109 rest on traced source.
- `GPUSpatialJoinPrepared` no longer skips the index build: it replays every bounds, BVH, sort and slab node on every encoding against a retained geometry copy (GEO-115). The ERG-02 latch problem is fixed by moving the commit onto the GPU, and that fix is what costs the build skip.
- Hilbert keys and the study-window latch were read by the lead: no defect. Grid generators: one EXTREME index-precision defect (GEO-109).
- The roadmap rows in this scope are mostly stale: GEOM-01, GEOM-02, LUM-23 and the "Triangulation twins" limit can be closed; LR-08, STD-08, GPU-12, GAP-01, DUP-01, GPU-05 and "GPU triangulation" need narrower text.
- No test, gate, GPU or browser was run. Every statement about a shader result is a lead unless marked EXECUTED-CPU.

## Refuted or downgraded

- Hilbert keys: the WGSL parallel-prefix curve matches the Wikipedia `xy2d` (0 mismatches over orders 1 to 8, sampled 9 to 16); no defect.
- Grid generators: shared vertices come from one lattice expression, so cells share bits; extent masks use exact orientation; no defect beyond GEO-109.
- Dot-density triangle CDF: the ordering proof in `dot-density-triangle-cdf.ts` holds (consecutive feature intervals, empty features give equal offsets); closes LUM-23.
- Dot-density count kernel (`ceil(scaled - u)`), per-slot clamp and the new `candidateOverflow` requirement: consistent; consumers do not hit the new `validateRequirement` at ordinary slot counts.
- `arrayLength(&view)` guards: bindings are sized to the view range (`wgsl-kernel-nodes.ts:402`), so they equal the view length.
- Bounded pair-sort key collision, zone anchors, pycnophylactic settle parity and conservation, points-on-line interpolation: refuted by the zonal slice.
- Ring shell probe midpoint rounding, overlay lattice at 1e7 (documented coarsening), Delaunay incircle at projected magnitudes (exact), rectangle-clip `requiredVertexCount` lower bound (documented): not defects.
- Overlay `overflow`/`candidateOverflow` consumers in the repo (`building-tessellation.ts:649`, `kepler/.../catalog-gpu-geometry.ts:800-826`, `repair-overlay.compute.ts:719`) read both flags: no stale reader found.
- Segment intersection degeneracies (collinear overlap, shared endpoint, T-junction, zero-length segments skipped upstream, uncertain predicates as kind 5): no defect in `segment-intersection-wgsl.ts:59-101`.
- Multipart join semantics (any-part, every-part, empty features, page seams, per-feature top-k): no defect found in source.
- Measures: serial and cooperative invalid-ring rules are equivalent for non-pole rings; hull group drop rule holds.

## Tests

**Oracle independence.** Strong: measures (pyproj and GeographicLib pins), label point (float64 polylabel plus analytic radii), topology (Shapely parity plus hand areas), the GEO-05 and GEO-06 pins (analytic), ring assembly (Python oracle), minimum clearance and coverage simplification (Shapely), areal interpolation (numpy and tobler fixtures), line points (`shapely.interpolate`), Hilbert (Wikipedia `xy2d` in `hilbert-oracle.ts`), grid generators (GeoPandas and turf fixtures). Same-reading oracles: GPU monotone triangulation (model, now duplicated, GEO-131), `dot-density-cpu.ts` (imports the slot limit and Philox from source, omits the zero-area rule), group geometry oracle, pycnophylactic numpy oracle (R `pycno` parity is the independent one).

**Weakest five.**
1. `GPUOffsetCurveComponents`: axis-aligned power-of-two fixtures only; the helper `offset-curve-components-test-utils.ts:50-95` requires equal `y` and accepts extra vertices, so GEO-101 and GEO-102 pass.
2. `GPUMinimumBounds` circles: no scale cases, and the only CPU check is the float64 twin (GEO-106).
3. `GPUSpatialPredicateJoin` pairs-only port: every spec wires `candidateOverflow`, so GEO-103 is invisible; the line-in-line `within` degenerate case (GEO-108) and near-tied nearest distances (float64 CPU rank versus float32 `frexp` rank) are untested.
4. `GPUZonalStatistics` variances: 14 rows of magnitude 0.5 to 100, at most 5 rows per feature, tolerance 1e-4 (GEO-105).
5. `GPUMakeValid` provenance and `GPUGeometryMeasures` groups: no two-overlapping-features-with-one-source-ID fixture (the group fix of this pass is not pinned); no geographic group test at all (GEO-104).

Other gaps: `GPUVertexSnap` (5 cases, no reuse or projected scale); packed group hull with groups of at most one triangle; pycnophylactic tolerance stop pinned only for the even remainder; `GPUGreatCircleArcs` with no antipodal, pole or +-180 case; `GPUVoronoiDiagram` with no cocircular case; `GPUGeometryValidity` with no projected-scale case.

**Discriminating-test rule.** Followed by GEO-02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08 and ERG-02 fixes; not followed by the GEO-01 fix beyond axis-aligned lines, the GEO-11 fix (no interior shared edges) and the make-valid group fix.
