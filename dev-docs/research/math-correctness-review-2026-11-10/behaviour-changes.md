# Behaviour changes of the math-fix pass (2026-11-10)

One record of what a caller of `@luma-contributors/analysis` sees after the fix pass, taken from
the `## Resolution (2026-11-10)` section of each of the 18 reports in this folder. Numbers are the
streams' own. Rows that only change words are listed under each table, not in it. Whether each
`default`, `throws` and `output` row is true in source, and which callers it reaches, is in
[fix-review-callers.md](./fix-review-callers.md).

Kinds:

- `result`: values change for some inputs.
- `default`: a default changed.
- `throws`: a new construction-time error.
- `status`: a status, validity or sentinel output changed meaning.
- `output`: a new optional prop or output.
- `seeded`: every seeded run changes.

"Callers affected" answers one question: does a caller who changes no code get a different result
or an error. "yes" means on ordinary input; "only if" names the input that triggers it.

## Most likely to surprise a caller

1. **Terrain row direction.** `GPUTerrainDerivatives`, `GPUTerrainCurvature`, `GPUReliefShading`,
   `GPUTerrainHorizon`, `GPUTerrainCastShadow`, `GPUTerrainVectorRuggedness` and `GPUGeomorphons`
   take `rowDirection` from `grid.affine[4]` when the prop is left out, and throw when an explicit
   value contradicts the grid.
2. **Terrain border.** `GPUTerrainDerivatives` and `GPUTerrainCurvature` default to
   `borderMode: 'one-sided'`. The outer ring of slope, aspect, hillshade and every curvature
   changes (border slope 5.71 to 11.31 degrees on the stream's plane). `GPUReliefShading` keeps
   `'clamp'`, so its default hillshade no longer equals the derivatives one in that ring.
3. **Every spatial cross-validation fold assignment changes**, for every seed
   (`GPUSpatialCrossValidation`, `GPUNestedInterpolationValidation`, `GPUSpatialCrossValidatedOLS`).
4. **Seeded draws change**: `GPUKMeans` with `'kmeans++'` (the default initialization),
   `GPUNetworkLouvain`, `GPUNetworkKFunction` envelopes, `GPUConditionalGaussianSimulation`,
   `GPUExpectationBasedSpaceTimeScan`. The distributions are the same; the numbers a given seed
   produces are not.
5. **`GPURasterReconstruction` floods through corners by default** (`connectivity` 8, was 4).
6. **Every equalized `GPURasterStretch` raster changes**, and percentile bounds move by up to one
   order statistic.
7. **`GPUNetworkNoding` topology**: a self-loop is listed once (`2 * edges - loops` entries), and
   unused weight slots hold NaN, not 3.4028234e38.
8. **Band intervals are `(a, b]`** in `GPUNetworkIsochrones` rasters and in the drive-time
   catchment recipe: a cost exactly on a break moves to the band below.
9. **`GPUCostDistance` rejects a sheared grid** at construction.
10. **`GPUNeighborSearch` inverse-distance weights**: the default `distanceFloor` is 1e-6, so
    coincident points become neighbours with weight `1e6^power` where they had weight 0.
11. **Same-type K of `GPUMarkedPointStatistics` grows by `n / (n - 1)`.**
12. **Sums and means are no longer quantised to 1/65536**: `GPUGroupStatistics` `sumValues` and
    `means`, `GPUKeyJoin` `'sum'` and `'mean'`, and the default sums of `GPUNetworkCoarsening`.
13. **Sentinels and statuses**: `GPUModalSplit` can write `0xffffffff` to `predictedModes`;
    `GPUTerrainFlowFractions` no-data rows are 0, not NaN; `GPUUtilizationVolumeIsopleths` volume of
    an empty cell is 1, not 0; `GPUProbitRegression` returns status 4 with NaN coefficients for a fit
    pinned on its bound; near-collinear regressions return status 2 instead of status 0.
14. **The trajectory acceleration flag marks different rows.** The bit value (2) is unchanged; a
    single displaced fix now gets it and the first good row after a jump no longer does.
15. **`addHotSpotAnalysisRecipe` throws** when `permutation` is passed with a `selfWeight` the
    permutation statistic cannot reproduce.
16. **`GPUDatumGridTransform` inverse** applies shifts below 1.1 m that the old default tolerance
    skipped.
17. **`GPUGeographicallyTemporallyWeightedRegression` AICc is larger** (the mgwr formula) and no
    longer equals mgtwr's `aicc`.
18. **`GPUCompositeScore`** returns the major component for negatively correlated indicators
    (loadings and scores change sign pattern).
19. **`GPUGeodesicPairs` `'rhumb'` midpoints** move in longitude (45 to 37.539 on the stream's pair).
20. **An infinite raster cell is no-data** in `GPURasterSampling`, `GPURasterProfile`,
    `GPUFocalStatistics` and `GPUChangeDetection`.
