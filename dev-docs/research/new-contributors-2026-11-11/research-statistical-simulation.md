# Statistical, simulation and optimization methods missing from the library (2026-11-11)

Scope: non-neural methods that suit WebGPU compute (matrix heavy, stencil heavy, Monte Carlo, many independent chains) and are not in the census (`census.md` in this folder), not a roadmap row, and not already excluded. Sizes and the one-second target come from the brief. Effort: S is days, M is one to two weeks, L is more (the roadmap's scale). Effort, float32 risk and verdicts are my judgment, not sourced claims.

## 0. What already exists that these build on

Read from the repository on 2026-11-11:

- **Published FFT.** `GPUFFT2D` takes `float32x2` complex views, sizes that are powers of two from 2 to 2048 per side, and a `batchCount` (`node_modules/@luma.gl/graph/dist/gpu-core/gpu-fft2d.d.ts:18-23`). In this repository only raster registration uses it (`raster-analysis/remote-sensing/gpu-raster-registration.ts`). So an exact circulant-embedding field, which needs a padded grid at least twice the field, tops out at 1024² per realization unless the field is tiled or made periodic.
- **Published PCG.** `GPUJacobiPCG` solves one symmetric positive-definite CSR system per instance, with an iteration upper bound, a squared-residual tolerance and a `breakdown` scalar (`gpu-pcg.d.ts`). No contributor uses it yet; nor `GPUMatMul`.
- **In this repository.** Philox random numbers with registered purposes (`analysis-core/kernels/philox-random.ts`); shared dense-solve and symmetric-eigen WGSL modules (roadmap STD-10; it also notes a missing QR module); `GPUConditionalGaussianSimulation` (exact, dense Cholesky, at most 4,096 observation and query rows combined); `GPUEnsembleScores` (CRPS, energy and variogram scores); `GPUMapColoring`; `GPUSpatialWeightsMinimumSpanningTree`; HMM Viterbi inside `GPUMapMatching`; `GPUTrajectoryKalman`; `GPUSinkhornDivergence`; `GPUWeightedOverlay` with per-frame weights; `GPURasterEOFProjection`, whose eigen step runs on the CPU after readback; `GPUSpatialInteraction`, which already does bounded iterative proportional fitting for OD margins; `GPUAZPRegions`, which states it has no tabu list or simulated annealing (`gpu-azp-regions.ts:165`).
- **Earlier verdicts.** The 2026-11-09 review ranked BYM2 by Laplace approximation (rank 12), DMD by randomized SVD (rank 10), CP/Tucker tensors (rank 11), Moran eigenvector filtering (rank 13) and HMM states with step selection (movement rank 10). None became a roadmap row. It also listed "full MCMC, full INLA" and "the simulations already excluded (flood, wildfire, urban growth)" as not recommended ([README](../spatiotemporal-review-2026-11-09/README.md), [research-spacetime-models.md](../spatiotemporal-review-2026-11-09/research-spacetime-models.md), [research-movement.md](../spatiotemporal-review-2026-11-09/research-movement.md)). This report keeps those exclusions unless it says why.

## 8. Other gaps noticed against PySAL, spatstat, GRASS, WhiteboxTools, ArcGIS, R-INLA and mgcv

- **Empirical Bayesian kriging (ArcGIS).** Subsets of about 100 points, about 100 simulated semivariograms each, predictions mixing them ([Esri help](https://resources.arcgis.com/en/help/main/10.1/0031/0031000000q9000000.htm)). Very parallel (subsets × simulations × refits), and the library has every part, but there is no open oracle. **Maybe**, low priority.
- **Soap-film smoothing (mgcv).** Needs a boundary basis and PDE solution over an irregular region ([Wood et al. 2008](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~swood34/simon/papers/soap.pdf)). **Skip.** Global thin-plate splines are already row IN05.
- **Gibbs point process simulation (spatstat `rmh`).** Birth-death Metropolis-Hastings is serial. **Skip.**
- **Poisson point process model with raster covariates** (spatstat `ppm`, the statistical core of presence-only habitat models). Quadrature plus a log-linear GLM by iteratively reweighted least squares: dense `GPUMatMul` over a million quadrature points. Not researched in depth here; **maybe**, and it is the inference half of the LGCP row.

## 10. What I could not verify

- Licences of GWmodel, adespatial, spatialreg, R `fields`, WhiteboxTools, G2S, emdi, ipfp, mipfp and rakeR were not confirmed in this session. R-INLA's licence is listed both as GPL (≥ 2) and as MIT on different builds. scikit-learn's BSD-3 licence was not re-fetched.
- I did not check whether the published `GPUSegmentedScan` accepts a custom associative operator (rank 8 depends on it) or whether `GPUMatMul` has size limits that would constrain rank 3.
- No timings were measured. Whether tens of PCG solves on 100,000 polygons, or 50 viewshed realizations on 1024², fit one second on an integrated GPU is unknown; each candidate needs a measurement before its limits are set.
- Several references were checked from abstracts, catalogue records or secondary citations only (Dietrich and Newsam, Wood and Chan, Mantoglou and Wilson, Finney, Clarke et al., Openshaw, Lilburne and Tarantola, Houtekamer and Zhang). The Wikipedia summary is used only for the Openshaw and Taylor title and the scale/zoning terminology.
- The closed-form Sobol indices for a linear overlay (section 7) are my derivation, not a sourced result; they assume independent weights and no renormalisation of weights to sum to one.
