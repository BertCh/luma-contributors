Flow models on origin-destination aggregation

Two contributors that work on zone-level flows: a parameter-free model that predicts flows from populations and distances, and a kernel smoother for observed flows. Both are reference-tier: work grows with the cube of the zone count, and construction rejects more than 1024 zones.

Both take zone centroids, planar or great-circle (spatialContext). Their inputs match what GPUFlowAggregation writes: zoneOutWeights is the outflows of the radiation model when the aggregation runs with excludeSelfFlows: true (T_i is the trips that leave zone i; without the option the zone totals also count trips that stay inside it), and the smoother reads flowOriginZoneIds, flowDestinationZoneIds, flowWeights and status.count (as validPairCount) directly.

GPURadiationFlowModel

The radiation model (Simini, Gonzalez, Maritan and Barabasi, Nature 2012). A trip leaving zone i is absorbed by zone j with probability m_i n_j / ((m_i + s_ij)(m_i + n_j + s_ij)), where m_i and n_j are the populations of the origin and destination and s_ij is the population of the zones strictly nearer to i than j, excluding both. The output is T_i times that probability, the expected flow, laid out flows[i * zoneCount + j] with a zero diagonal. It draws no random integers, unlike scikit-mobility's Radiation.generate.

Prop Meaning
positions, populations, outflows One row per zone. outflows is T_i, the trips leaving each zone, not counting trips that stay inside it
normalization 'none' (default), or 'finite-size', which divides by 1 - m_i / M so every row sums to T_i
output.flows zoneCount^2 rows

Ties in distance: zones at exactly the distance of the destination do not count in s_ij. Implementations that sort by distance count one of two tied zones and not the other, so they differ there.

Verified against a hand-computed three-zone case, against a binary64 reference that sorts the zones per origin and takes a prefix sum of population (a different route from the kernel), and against the closed-form row sum T_i (1 - m_i / M). scikit-mobility was not run.

GPUKernelFlowSmoothing

Smoothing of OD flows: each flow is re-estimated as the kernel-weighted mean of the flows between neighbouring origins and neighbouring destinations. Smoothing over neighbourhoods of both ends is the idea of Guo and Zhu (IEEE TVCG 2014); this contributor does not reproduce their estimator. The definition used:

  1. The neighbourhood of zone i is every zone within the smallest distance h_i at which the locationWeights of the zones within h_i (zone i included) sum to at least neighborhoodSize. Ties at h_i are included.
  2. A neighbour k has the Gaussian weight exp(-d_ik^2 / (2 sigma_i^2)) with sigma_i = bandwidthRatio * h_i; K_ii = 1.
  3. S_ij = sum_a sum_b K_ia K_jb M_ab / (sum_a K_ia * sum_b K_jb). Pairs without a flow count as zero.

This is a normalised kernel mean, so it does not conserve the total flow. Each row of weights is divided by its own sum; the columns are not. A zone that is a neighbour of many zones spreads its flows into all of them and the total grows, and a zone that is nobody's neighbour loses part of its flows. With zones at x = 0, 1 and 10, unit weights, neighborhoodSize 2 and bandwidthRatio 0.5, one flow of 100 inside the middle zone sums to 125.3 after smoothing and one flow of 100 inside the far zone sums to 77.6. Rescale the output when totals must match the input.

The neighbourhood size, the kernel and the bandwidth ratio are this library's own choices; the paper was not available to check them and no packaged implementation exists. The tests use a binary64 reference written from the three points above, the three-zone case worked by hand, and closed-form limits (a zero-size neighbourhood returns the input; a very wide kernel returns the mean). Flow selection, which removes duplicate smoothed flows, is not provided.

Prop Meaning
flows {kind: 'matrix', matrix} with zoneCount^2 rows, or {kind: 'pairs', originZoneIds, destinationZoneIds, weights, validPairCount} as written by GPUFlowAggregation (distinct pairs)
locationWeights Weight of each zone in the neighbourhood size
parameters Per-frame, getGPUKernelFlowSmoothingParameterValues: neighbourhood size, bandwidth ratio
output.smoothed zoneCount^2 rows
output.radii Optional neighbourhood radius of each zone

GPUKernelFlowSmoothing parameters

  • Packer: getGPUKernelFlowSmoothingParameterValues(settings), writes GPU_KERNEL_FLOW_SMOOTHING_PARAMETER_LENGTH (4) float32 values.

Settings of getGPUKernelFlowSmoothingParameterValues:

Field Type Description
neighborhoodSize number Weighted size p of a zone's neighbourhood, in the unit of locationWeights: the nearest zones are taken until their weights sum to at least p. A value of 0 or less keeps only the zone itself (and zones at distance 0), which reproduces the input.
bandwidthRatio (optional) number Gaussian standard deviation as a fraction of the neighbourhood radius: sigma = ratio * radius. Defaults to 0.5.
