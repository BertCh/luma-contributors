Lagrangian diagnostics (time-varying fields, FTLE, connectivity, residence time)

Particles integrated through a time-varying vector field, with FTLE, raster ridge extraction, connectivity and residence time. Planar inputs use the field's own distance units. Geographic inputs are projected internally through published luma.gl projection programs. Tests include NumPy-generated ridge and geographic-flow fixtures; OceanParcels was not run.

GPULagrangianFlowMap

Takes one second-order Runge-Kutta (midpoint) step p' = p + h v(p + h/2 v(p, t), t + h/2), stepCount times, for every seed. The field has two slices, velocitiesStart valid at sliceStartTime and velocitiesEnd at sliceEndTime; the velocity at time t is their linear blend with weight (t - sliceStartTime) / (sliceEndTime - sliceStartTime) clamped to [0, 1], so the field is held constant outside the interval. To integrate a longer record, swap the slices and slice times between encodings. A negative timeStep integrates backward. Spatial sampling is the bilinear sampler of GPUParticleAdvection.

There is no respawn, no randomness and no atomic; a run is bitwise reproducible.

Set spatialContext: {coordinateSpace: 'longitude-latitude', metric: 'none'} for geographic fields. Seeds, fieldExtent and final output.positions are longitude/latitude degrees. The two velocity components are Web Mercator projected metres per time unit. The contributor projects each seed into EPSG:3857, integrates there, and inversely projects each RK2 sampling position before sampling the degree grid. Ground east/north velocities must be transformed to that projected frame by the caller. There is no implicit geodesic motion, longitude wrapping or pole clamping: projection-rejected positions are lost. Continuous longitudes must stay inside [-180, 180] and latitudes inside the Web Mercator square-world domain (about ±85.05113°). Absolute projected float32 positions lose metre-scale detail at continental offsets; this mode is unsuitable for sub-metre movement there.

spatialContext, field dimensions, step count and optional outputs are compile-time. Input values and the parameter view are per-frame. output.validity optionally writes one flag per seed, including zero for projection rejection. Lost particles also retain the existing NaN positions.

Row direction: the field slices place row 0 at the smallest y of fieldExtent and v is the velocity toward larger y. No grid affine is read and nothing is checked at construction, so a north-up field (row 0 at the top) must have its row order reversed before upload. Following a grid affine is deferred (roadmap 12.4). The seed grid that GPUFiniteTimeLyapunovExponent reads needs no reordering: output row r belongs to seed row r, and the exponent is the same whether seed rows advance toward larger or smaller y.

Prop Meaning
seeds float32x2 start positions
velocitiesStart, velocitiesEnd Packed (u, v) rows, fieldWidth * fieldHeight, row 0 at the smallest y
parameters Per-frame, getGPULagrangianFlowMapParameterValues: field extent, start time, signed time step, slice times
stepCount Compile-time, 1 to 8192: the bounded per-thread loop
regions Optional uint32 region of each field cell, GPU_LAGRANGIAN_NO_REGION outside every region
spatialContext Planar by default, or longitude-latitude with metric none
output.validity Optional explicit flag: 1 for a valid final position, otherwise 0
output.positions Final positions. NaN for a particle that left the field or reached a NaN cell
output.regionClasses Start regions then end regions, period-major (needs regions)
output.residenceTimes Time in the start region before its first exit (needs regions)

Residence time is |timeStep| times the number of steps that began inside the start region, so its resolution is one step. A particle that never leaves reports stepCount * |timeStep| (censored); a particle that leaves the field counts as having exited; a seed outside every region reports 0.

GPUFiniteTimeLyapunovExponent

FTLE = ln(sqrt(lambdaMax(J^T J))) / |T|, where J is the gradient of the final positions with respect to the seeds on a regular seed grid. Central differences are used in the interior and one-sided differences on the border; lambdaMax is the closed-form larger eigenvalue of the 2 by 2 matrix. The result is NaN where a particle in the stencil is lost. Integrate with a negative time step for the backward exponent.

For geographic final positions, pass the same longitude-latitude spatial context. The stencil projects them before differencing, so the original seeds must form a regular grid in projected metres, with seedSpacingX/Y in those metres. A regular degree grid does not meet that requirement. Optional input validity rejects the center and every stencil that uses it; optional output.validity identifies finite FTLE results.

Verified on a linear strain field u = e x, v = -e y, where the exponent is e everywhere, on a one-directional stretch (forward e, backward 0), and on a strain rate that ramps linearly across the two slices, where the exponent is the time mean of the rate (a frozen first slice gives 0).

GPUScalarFieldRidges

{/* lens:GPUScalarFieldRidges */}

::lens{scenario=gpu-raster.scalar-field-ridges rung=result caption="Quadratic field: the half-cell tie selects column 3; missing samples invalidate their stencils"} ::lens{scenario=gpu-raster.scalar-field-ridges rung=formula caption="Negative Hessian curvature and a normal Newton step inside the half-open pixel box"} ::lens{scenario=gpu-raster.scalar-field-ridges rung=datum datum=24 caption="Cell 24: column 3 owns the exact half-cell ridge tie"} ::lens{scenario=gpu-raster.scalar-field-ridges rung=kernel caption="The central difference stencil, smallest eigenpair and subcell ridge test"} ::lens{scenario=gpu-raster.scalar-field-ridges rung=graph caption="A fixed-size ridge mask with stencil validity and optional curvature strength"}

The synthetic lens uses f = 20 - (column - 2.5)^2 on a 7 × 7 metre grid. Its normal curvature is 2. The half-open pixel rule selects column 3 in interior rows 1 through 5. Missing cell 40 (column 5, row 5) invalidates every stencil touching it; border outputs are also zero.

Extracts a fixed-sized ridge mask from a scalar GPURasterBand, including Brownian-bridge utilization densities, cumulative utilization volumes and FTLE. The grid must have orthogonal pixel axes and at least 3 × 3 cells. Rotated uniform grids work; geographic and Web Mercator ground-distance modes require axis-aligned grids. The parameter packer reads the grid affine, so update the parameter view when the affine changes. Dimensions and cellSizeMode are compile-time. Geographic grids require a longitude-latitude spatial context with metric none and cellSizeMode: 'geographic'; otherwise the context is planar.

A central 3 × 3 difference stencil gives the gradient and Hessian in the orthogonal pixel basis. The smallest Hessian eigenvalue defines downward curvature and its eigenvector the ridge normal. Spacing quotients combine mantissas and integer exponents without forming a spacing product, so a representable curvature survives even when the squared cell spacing would overflow. The shared 2 × 2 eigenpair helper retains the smaller diagonal in its original units and subtracts a rationalized Jacobi correction; only its bounded direction calculation is scaled. This avoids half-trace cancellation and preserves a small normal negative eigenvalue beside a much larger positive diagonal. Subnormal inputs and results may still be flushed to zero by the GPU. A cell is selected when curvature is strictly positive and meets minimumCurvature, its calibrated value meets minimumValue, and the normal Newton displacement falls inside its pixel box [-0.5, 0.5) along both axes. Exact half-cell ties go to the larger column/row; equal Hessian eigenvalues choose the column normal. This is a local raster approximation of the height-ridge criterion of Peikert and Sadlo (2008), with no curve tracing, smoothing, or claim that every detected ridge is a material LCS.

output.ridges and output.validity are required uint32 views, one row per cell. output.strength optionally holds max(0, -smallestEigenvalue) at every valid stencil. Borders and stencils touching missing or nonfinite samples write zero to every output. Band calibration, no-data and validity are honoured. Flat plateaus and valleys have no ridges. Geographic and Web Mercator modes use the existing row-local WGS84 ground scale; their Hessian is a local tangent approximation. Outputs preserve the source cell index and never truncate, so no compact-output capacity or overflow status is needed. There is no CPU operation twin for these raster contributors.

Connectivity matrix

Compose GPULagrangianFlowMap (with regions and output.regionClasses) with GPUTransitionMatrix using periodCount: 2 and classCount equal to the number of regions (at most 64). Its counts are the connectivity matrix counts[from * K + to] and its probabilities are the row-normalized matrix. Particles that end outside every region, including lost ones, are not counted; output.ignored of the transition matrix counts them.
