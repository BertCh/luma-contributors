Statistical definitions and bounded algorithm contracts are documented in Statistical expansion, Expansion recipes, and the raster and network expansion references.

GPUConcaveHull

Triangle reservation is bounded by 715,827,882 to keep six boundary words per triangle within uint32 addressing; device bindings impose lower limits.

An alpha complex selects Delaunay triangles by maximum circumradius and derives the exposed boundary edges; graph and validity are the appropriate structural explanation. The circumcircle solve independently scales its two edge equations by powers of two, protecting products at large, small and unequal coordinate scales. Degenerate triangles and unrepresentable final radii are excluded. Float32 accuracy still limits nearly collinear circumcircles.

GPUGroupConcaveHull

Independent alpha complexes preserve original point IDs and empty group slots; boundary and triangle capacities remain explicit.

The default layout: 'fixed' reserves T = max(0, 2*n-5) triangle rows per group and 3*T boundary-edge rows per group, where n is the total input position count. Validity masks select retained rows. With layout: 'packed', reserve only T triangle rows and 3*T boundary-edge rows across all groups. Each list is compact and group-major; its validity is one in the used prefix and zero in padding. output.groups.triangleOffsets, triangleCounts, edgeOffsets and edgeCounts each have groupCount rows and describe ranges in the corresponding compact list. An empty or fully removed group has count zero and the current prefix offset. When the total input has fewer than three positions, reserve zero geometry rows; packed counts and every group range are still written as zero. Resubmitting after all triangles are removed clears the old compact geometry, validity and counts. Zero-length logical views still need one physical row of backing storage for the published binding helper: 12 bytes for uint32x3 triangles, 8 for uint32x2 edges, and 4 for scalar validity.

Packed output.status.count and requiredCount count boundary-edge rows, and overflow is zero because this layout requires the full linear reservation. output.status.converged still has one row per group: a nonconverged group contributes no triangles or edges. The packed reservation is bounded by 715,827,882 total triangle rows for uint32 addressing, with device bindings imposing lower limits. Both layouts use the same group-aware Delaunay build and retain original position rows. All output fields, including the four packed range views, must use distinct buffers disjoint from the inputs. Missing packed fields fail at construction with a contributor-prefixed diagnostic. The grouped contributor snapshots its layout and nested output maps at construction while retaining the supplied GPU views; later edits to the caller's props/maps do not change the validated graph.
