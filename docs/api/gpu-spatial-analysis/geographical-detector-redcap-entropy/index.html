Geographical detector, REDCAP, spatial entropy and the contiguity-order correlogram

STX-08, first group. Import from @luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-spatial-analysis. Each method names its reference and what was run against it. Where a reference package could not be installed, a binary64 CPU evaluation of the published definition stands in, and the section says so.

Geographical detector: GPUGeographicalDetector

The factor detector of Wang et al. (2010): q = 1 - SSW / SST, the share of the variance of a response values explained by a stratification labels (a classification, an administrative code, a zoning). It reads the sums of squares, strata sizes and strata means from GPURegionPartitionEvaluation (which gained an optional output.regionMeans for this), then reduces the strata means in one tiled pass. No float atomics; results are bitwise reproducible.

output.summary is laid out by GPU_GEOGRAPHICAL_DETECTOR_LAYOUT: q, fStatistic F = ((N - L) / (L - 1)) q / (1 - q), nonCentrality, strataCount L, observationCount N, the two degrees of freedom and totalSumOfSquares. Rows labelled 0xffffffff or at or above labelCapacity, and rows whose value is NaN or infinite, are left out. A constant response gives NaN.

The p-value is computed on the host, from the read-back summary: getGeographicalDetectorPValue(summary) is the upper tail of the non-central F distribution, summed as a Poisson mixture of incomplete beta functions in binary64 (getNonCentralFUpperTail is the distribution tail itself). Both beta arguments are formed directly, retaining the smaller argument even when the other rounds to one. Stable log-beta normalization handles disparate degrees of freedom; weighted survival terms are accumulated in log space and rounded only after summation, including subnormal tails. For finite positive F, supported non-centrality is at most 10000; larger or infinite non-centrality throws an error. With valid positive degrees of freedom and finite non-centrality, F = Infinity returns zero and F <= 0 returns one before applying that mixture limit. This includes a perfect detector (q = 1) with non-centrality above 10000. NaN statistics return NaN. Each mixture direction and beta continued fraction is bounded by 100000 iterations; a convergence failure throws an error. It is not computed on the GPU.

For consumers that can display a summary without inference, readGeographicalDetectorPValue(summary) returns {pValue, unavailableReason}. A supported result has a numeric pValue and null reason; an undefined statistic, unsupported range or numerical convergence failure has null pValue and an explanatory reason. Unrelated read errors still throw. The strict helper above keeps its throwing contract. Kepler preserves all summary rows, publishes null in the pValue row, sets summary.pValueStatus = 'unavailable' and reports a geographical-detector-p-value-unavailable warning with the reason. Supported probabilities have pValueStatus = 'available'. The website readout likewise retains q and F and labels an unavailable probability with its reason. For example, strata [A,B,B,B] with responses [1000,1001,1002,1003] retain q = 0.6 and F = 3 even though their non-centrality exceeds 10000.

Reference: geodetector 0.2.0 (MIT) q_statistic and q_significance_test, whose formulas match the R GD package; its _stats.py ran in stx08-spatial-methods.py. Differences from the reference, stated as they are:

  • The package drops strata with a single row. This contributor keeps them (they add nothing to SSW but count in L and N). Compare on data whose strata have at least two rows.
  • nonCentrality is the published formula (sum_h mean_h^2 - (sum_h sqrt(N_h) mean_h)^2 / N) / sigma^2 with the population variance. It is not invariant to shifting values, and it differs from the textbook sum_h N_h (mean_h - mean)^2 / sigma^2. It was implemented as the reference computes it, not corrected.
  • float32 accuracy: q agrees to about 2e-5 in the fixture. nonCentrality subtracts nearly equal sums when the strata means dwarf their spread (the fixture's offset case, means near 50), and keeps one to two digits there; centre and scale values first when that matters, bearing in mind the formula is not shift invariant.

REDCAP single linkage: GPURedcapSingleLinkage

Contiguity-constrained single-linkage clustering, cut at a region count that is a per-frame parameter. It is the tree of first-order single-linkage REDCAP (Guo 2008) with a different partition step, so it does not return the REDCAP regions: REDCAP partitions the tree by removing, at each step, the edge whose removal lowers the within-region sum of squared deviations the most, which for this tree is what GPUSpatialWeightsMinimumSpanningTree followed by GPUSkaterRegions computes; this class removes the heaviest edges. On a path with values [0, 1, 2.1, 3.3, 4.6, 6.0] and two regions it returns {0..4}, {5} (within-region sum of squares 13.26) where the SSD partition is {0, 1, 2}, {3, 4, 5} (5.85). Single linkage chains, so expect single outlying rows to be split off first. Use SKATER when REDCAP regions are wanted. The minimum spanning tree of the contiguity graph under squared Euclidean dissimilarity of the standardised attributes holds every single-linkage merge, so removing its k - c heaviest edges (c trees in the forest) leaves k contiguous regions. It composes GPUSpatialWeightsMinimumSpanningTree (edges and dissimilarities), a stable GPUSort on (cost, edge slot), a masked copy of the weights and the weak components of what remains (createMaskedWeakComponentNodes). Region k refines region k - 1.

parameters holds the region count (getGPURedcapSingleLinkageParameterValues). Outputs: labels (the smallest row index of each region), and optionally regionCount and status with converged, iterationLimitReached and invalidCount (the tree's non-finite entry count; an edge that touches a non-finite row costs +Infinity and is cut first). The independent optional status.iterationCount receives the number of executed label-propagation rounds: always componentIterations, because all scheduled rounds run even after labels stabilize. It excludes the spanning-tree stage and is written even for a partition with no retained edges; requesting it does not require either convergence output. status.invalidCount requires a packed uint32 view with at least one word, validated at construction. Region count, convergence/iteration-limit flags and invalid count write only their first word; spare scalar capacity remains untouched on ordinary, suppressed and recovery frames.

A supplied nonzero weights.status.overflow or candidateOverflow suppresses the partition: every label is 0xffffffff, regionCount is 0, and both convergence/iteration-limit flags are 0. The executed iteration count and the attribute invalid count retain their own meanings. When the producer restores complete weights and clears the loss flag, the next submission publishes the ordinary partition again; a partial forest is never presented as a valid region assignment. REDCAP passes the original CSR and both supplied loss words directly to its minimum-spanning-tree stage. That stage suppresses its tree flags and edge count on the GPU; REDCAP also suppresses the dependent partition outputs. Clean status words produce the same partition as weights without status, and changing either word requires no graph recompilation. REDCAP snapshots its declared weights, loss views and output maps before validation. Replacing the caller's maps after construction cannot disconnect a validated loss port; change buffer contents to resubmit with the same bindings.

{/* lens:GPURedcapSingleLinkage */}

Limits and non-goals:

  • The label propagation runs componentIterations iterations (default 32, at most 256) and output.status.converged reports whether that sufficed. A 300-row path with regions of 100 rows converged in 32; a region that is a much longer thin path may need more, and an unconverged labelling is not valid.
  • No minimum region size or bound (the GeoDa and spopt floor), and no full-order, average or complete linkage variants (they need the tree rebuilt per region). Weights must be symmetric.
  • Ties among edge costs are broken by the lower edge slot; other libraries tie-break differently, so compare on distinct costs.
  • float32: costs are float32 squared distances, so two edges whose true costs differ by about one part in 1e7 can swap order.

Reference: no REDCAP package was pinned or run (spopt 0.8.0 has none). The reference is the constrained single-linkage definition, evaluated in binary64 with SciPy (minimum_spanning_tree, the k - c heaviest edges removed, connected_components); it is the same partition as sklearn.cluster.AgglomerativeClustering(linkage='single', connectivity=W), which was not run. The fixture has a 7 by 7 and a 9 by 5 lattice and a two-component graph, at region counts from 1 to above the row count.

Field Type Description

Spatial entropy: GPUSpatialEntropy and GPUCategoryPairCounts

GPUCategoryPairCounts counts, over the links of a weights graph, the ordered category pairs and the rows per category, with exact integer atomics (at most 32 categories). GPUSpatialEntropy builds on it: the Shannon entropy of the categories, the entropy of the origin and destination category of a link, the co-occurrence entropy of the ordered pair (Leibovici 2009), and the spatial mutual information H(origin) + H(destination) - H(pair) (Altieri, Cocchi and Roli 2018), with its share of the destination entropy. All in nats. Every active slot (positive weight, another row: the shared neighbor rule) is a link; self slots and zero-weight slots are not, and the size of a positive weight is not used: use contiguity weights, or distance-band weights per distance class for the distance-class form. output.summary is laid out by GPU_SPATIAL_ENTROPY_LAYOUT.

Entropies are float32 from exact counts and agree with binary64 to about 2e-6. Batty's area-weighted entropy and the Altieri conditional terms are not implemented. Reference: the R package spatentropy (GPL) was not installed, so the reference is a binary64 NumPy evaluation of the definitions above (stx08-spatial-methods.py).

Correlogram over contiguity orders: addContiguityOrderCorrelogramRecipe

Global Moran's I with analytic inference for the neighbors at exactly 1 to maximumOrder steps: per order GPUSpatialWeightsAlgebra higherOrder, GPUSpatialWeightsTransform 'row' and GPUGlobalSpatialStatistics. outputs.correlogram holds GPU_CONTIGUITY_ORDER_CORRELOGRAM_COLUMNS.length floats per order (I, expectation, both variances, z and p under normality and randomization, n, island count). outputs.incompleteOrders is one flag per order; it includes upstream final/candidate loss and either loss introduced by that order. A flagged order publishes NaN Moran statistics and inference. Clearing input loss and resubmitting the same graph restores defined results when the order fits. Capacities grow with the order: the defaults (slotCapacity and workCapacity) hold a square lattice, and memory is maximumOrder times the order capacity.

Reference: a binary64 breadth-first ring construction and Moran's I with the esda.Moran expectation and variances, in stx08-spatial-methods.py; libpysal and esda were not installed. The fixture has an 8 by 8 and a 10 by 6 lattice for orders 1 to 5. The reference excludes rows with no neighbor at an order, as islands: 'exclude' does; no fixture exercises an island order.
