Geometry validity & intersections

GPUSegmentIntersection

Finds every intersecting pair of line segments within one geometry, with the kind and point of each intersection.

::lens{scenario=gpu-spatial-analysis.segment-intersection rung=result caption="1,615 vertices of 721 street edges; points mark where segments meet or cross, coloured by kind."}

Maximum: each side has at most getGPUSegmentIntersectionMaximumVertexCount(device) (4194304 on a default device: the 32-byte segment-table row per vertex must fit one storage binding) positions rows; construction throws segment-intersection left.positions rows must be at most 4194304 above it.

Planar only: spatialContext defaults to native planar coordinates and a longitude-latitude context throws; project to a planar frame first (createGPULocalFrame, GPULocalFrameProjection). Orientation signs are exact.

Finds every intersecting pair of line segments between two geometries (left, right) or within one (left only), like turf lineIntersect and kinks, self-intersection tests and noding points. Each side is a tagged line or polygon geometry port (kind: 'lines' or 'polygons', one kind per side). A segment is identified by the index of its start vertex. Polygon rings close implicitly; rings with fewer than three vertices, zero-length segments and segments with non-finite coordinates are skipped.

Prop Meaning
left, right? Geometries. Omit right for self mode (left < right, adjacent segments skipped)
sameFeatureOnly? Self mode: only same-feature pairs (per-feature self-intersection and ring crossings)
output.pairs {leftRows, rightRows, status: {count, overflow, candidateOverflow?, requiredCount?}}, sorted by (left, right); capacity is leftRows.length
output.kinds? GPU_SEGMENT_INTERSECTION_KIND: proper 1, touch 2, collinearTouch 3, overlap 4, uncertain 5
output.points?, output.endPoints? The intersection point; for an overlap, the two ends of the shared span
output.leftFeatures?, output.rightFeatures?, output.leftRings?, output.rightRings? Feature and ring (or line) rows of each segment
output.status.uncertainCount? Count of uncertain pairs within capacity
spatialSort? Morton sort before the BVH build; on by default from 1024 vertices; the output is identical
leafCapacity? Power-of-two BVH leaves, by default the next power of two of the right vertex count

proper means the interiors cross at one point. touch is one shared point where an endpoint of one lies on the other (non-parallel), collinearTouch is collinear with one shared endpoint, and overlap is collinear sharing positive length. The pipeline is a segment table, a GPUBVH over the right segments, a counted and scanned probe, exact classification and a bounded sorted output; nothing is read back. On overflow the output keeps a sorted prefix (status.count = min(total, capacity), status.overflow = 1, status.requiredCount unclamped), and the columns beyond status.count are unspecified. status.uncertainCount and the classification columns cover pairs within capacity only. A collinear chain of n segments lists all n^2 overlap pairs. output.pairs.status.overflow describes final pair capacity only. BVH leaf loss is independent, reported in output.pairs.status.candidateOverflow. Both supplied words are rewritten on each encoding; requiredCount is a lower bound when BVH leaves were lost. On clean reuse both flags are zero. If leafCapacity is below the indexed geometry's vertex-slot count, candidateOverflow is required: actual segment counts are GPU data, so construction cannot guarantee those slots suffice. Nested consumers that omit the diagnostic must use enough BVH slots or construction refuses that configuration. GPUPairGather preserves both causes. Geometry descriptors, options, pair/column maps and nested status destinations are captured at construction. Removing or replacing a caller-map field later cannot change the validated bindings; the contents of the retained buffers remain dynamic.

graph.add(new GPUSegmentIntersection({
  left: {kind: 'lines', positions, lineOffsets},
  output: {pairs: {leftRows, rightRows, status: {count, overflow}}, kinds, points}
}));

GPUGeometryValidity

Point and line inputs are single-part feature ports. Multipart point/line featureOffsets are rejected before output row validation; use feature-aware joins or measures when grouping is needed.

Computes per-feature diagnostic issue codes and optional nonzero-usable validity for polygons, lines or points.

::lens{scenario=gpu-spatial-analysis.geometry-validity rung=result caption="102 park polygons shaded by their issue codes; the orientation convention is ignored."}

Maximum: polygons.positions has at most getGPUSegmentIntersectionMaximumVertexCount(device) (4194304 on a default device: the 32-byte segment-table row per vertex must fit one storage binding) rows.

Planar only: spatialContext defaults to native planar coordinates and a longitude-latitude context throws; project to a planar frame first (createGPULocalFrame, GPULocalFrameProjection). Orientation signs are exact.

A per-feature validity bitmask for polygons and multipolygons (the four-level GPUPolygonGeometryPort layout), like PostGIS ST_IsValidDetail and Shapely explain_validity. output.issueCode holds diagnostic bits; decodeGPUGeometryValidityIssueCode returns named flags and hasStructuralIssue, which describes observed evidence only. A zero issue code cannot certify validity when checks are incomplete. Optional output.validity is 1 when the implemented structural checks pass, otherwise 0. Orientation and repeated-vertex advisories leave it 1. Polygon intersection overflow suppresses every validity row because the missing intersections cannot be assigned to particular features. This flag does not certify the unsupported checks listed below. Breaking (2026-11-17): rename output.mask to output.issueCode; feed output.validity to downstream input masks. Diagnostic and status output ranges must not overlap one another or retained geometry inputs. Supplied status.overflow and status.intersectionCount scalars are reset to zero on every point/line submission because those kinds do not perform intersection checks. Kepler marks zero-issue rows incomplete when polygon intersection overflow persists after retries. The explorer counts published validity rows, including advisory-only rows, and withholds its valid polygon count while checks are incomplete. The website hover and showcase map use the published usability rows too: an unobserved structural defect is not a pass. With zero issue code and usability zero they show an incomplete check; observed structural defects remain visible. The showcase withholds “all valid” on overflow. The website preserves issue bit 256 as uncertain in hover text and uses a separate “uncertain predicate” class. It does not label an uncertifiable predicate as a repeated vertex. The geometry-validity result view explicitly selects the resultParks polygon capture; full results retain overflow and intersectionCount as separate scalar diagnostics below the map.

The bits of GPU_GEOMETRY_VALIDITY_BIT are nonFinite 1, unclosedRing 2 (explicit closure only), shortRing 4 (fewer than 3 vertices once a closing duplicate is dropped; rows are counted, so A, B, B, A does not set it and fails through selfIntersection instead), repeatedVertex 8, selfIntersection 16, crossingRings 32 (a proper crossing or overlap between different rings of one feature; point touches are allowed), holeOutsideShell 64, badOrientation 128 and uncertain 256. GPU_GEOMETRY_VALIDITY_STRUCTURAL_MASK is every bit except badOrientation (a convention) and repeatedVertex (GEOS and OGC accept repeated vertices, so it is reported but does not make a geometry structurally invalid).

Prop Meaning
polygons, output.issueCode, output.validity? The input, diagnostic code and optional nonzero-usable flag (featureCount rows each)
intersectionCapacity Capacity of the internal same-feature intersection list; valid data produces almost none
output.status.overflow, output.status.intersectionCount? The overflow flag (the self-intersection and crossing bits may then be incomplete) and the unclamped count
ringClosure? 'implicit' (default) or 'explicit', which sets unclosedRing
orientation? 'counter-clockwise-shell' (default), 'clockwise-shell' or 'ignore'

lines ({kind: 'lines', positions, lineOffsets}) and points replace polygons for the other kinds (Shapely is_valid, is_valid_reason on lines and points). Lines report nonFinite and tooFewPoints, points report nonFinite; neither needs an intersection list.

Not checked: interior connectivity (holes touching at two points), nested holes, nested polygons of a multipolygon, and rings that leave the shell only through boundary vertices. Repeated vertices are reported although OGC accepts them. Hole containment tests one off-boundary vertex per hole.

graph.add(new GPUGeometryValidity({
  polygons,
  output: {issueCode, validity, status: {overflow}},
  intersectionCapacity: 1024
}));

GPUGeometryPredicates

Evaluates per-feature predicates and counts such as simplicity, vertex count and hole count for points, lines or polygons. geometry and other use GPUGeometryPredicatesGeometry: one point or one linestring per row, or the polygon/multipolygon hierarchy. Multipart point/line featureOffsets are rejected at construction, including ports supplied through spreads or casts; this contributor does not yet reduce those parts into feature-level predicate/count columns.

::lens{scenario=gpu-spatial-analysis.geometry-predicates rung=result caption="102 park polygons shaded by stored vertex count; simplicity and hole counts are captured alongside."}

Maximum: with isSimple or isRing, geometry.positions has at most getGPUSegmentIntersectionMaximumVertexCount(device) (4194304 on a default device: the 32-byte segment-table row per vertex must fit one storage binding) rows.

Planar only: spatialContext defaults to native planar coordinates and a longitude-latitude context throws; project to a planar frame first (createGPULocalFrame, GPULocalFrameProjection).

Per-feature columns for points, linestrings or polygons (Shapely is_simple, is_ring, is_closed, is_ccw, get_num_points, count_coordinates, get_num_interior_rings, get_num_geometries, extract_unique_points, equals_exact, equals_identical). Each output is optional and uint32 (0 or 1 for booleans), all in the output bag: isSimple, isRing, isClosed, isCcw, numPoints, numCoordinates, numInteriorRings and numGeometries.

  • isSimple and isRing use GPUSegmentIntersection in self mode, so they need intersectionCapacity and output.status.overflow; features after an overflow can be wrong. A polygon is simple when no ring meets itself (Shapely 2.1). isCcw follows the GEOS Orientation::isCCW rule (the JTS 1.19 flat-top algorithm) for linestrings and is 0 for other kinds, as in Shapely.
  • other (same kind and feature count) enables equalsExact (per-frame tolerance through getGPUGeometryPredicatesParameterValues) and equalsIdentical, which compare feature i with feature i. The distance is f32.
  • output.uniquePositions and output.uniqueOffsets give extract_unique_points: first-occurrence order, as Shapely, with -0 equal to 0.
  • Limits: one thread per feature in the row-wise kernels (a huge single feature is serial); the self-mode look-ahead is 1024; an empty polygon gives 0 geometries where Shapely gives 1. Open: equals_exact(normalize=True), multilinestring and multipoint layouts.

GPUMinimumClearance

Measures the smallest distance a single vertex could move before each feature becomes invalid, and which vertex sets it.

::lens{scenario=gpu-spatial-analysis.minimum-clearance rung=result caption="102 park polygons shaded by minimum clearance in metres."}

Maximum: geometry.positions has at most getGPUSegmentIntersectionMaximumVertexCount(device) (4,194,304 on a default device) rows, because the 32-byte segment table per vertex must fit one 128 MiB binding.

leafCapacity (optional, a power of two) sets the BVH leaf slots over the segments and defaults to the next power of two of the vertex count, which never overflows. A smaller value drops the trailing segments from the BVH (in Morton order when spatialSort is on), so clearances may come out too large. To detect this, pass output.status.candidateOverflow, a one-row uint32 view that receives 1 when segments were dropped and 0 otherwise. Without that view an undersized leafCapacity is silent.

Planar only: a longitude/latitude spatialContext throws at construction; project first with createGPULocalFrame and GPULocalFrameProjection. It measures distance, so local-lambert-equal-area frames throw.

Per-feature shapely.minimum_clearance and minimum_clearance_line for linestrings and (multi)polygons: the smallest distance one vertex could move to make the feature invalid, as the minimum over vertices of the distance to every other vertex and to every segment that does not have that vertex as an endpoint (GEOS rule, repeated points ignored). Props: geometry (lines or polygons), and an output with at least one of clearances (float32, Infinity when none), lines (float32x4, vertex and nearest point, NaN when none) and vertexIds (uint32 row of the fragile vertex, 0xffffffff when none). A segment table and BVH feed one branch-and-bound probe per vertex, restricted to its own feature, then one 64-lane workgroup per feature reduces its vertex range to the lexicographic minimum of (distance, vertex row) with a fixed tree and no atomics, so results are deterministic and ties go to the lowest vertex row. Parity with Shapely 2.1.2 is within 2e-5 relative on 52 multipolygons and 69 linestrings; line end points can come out in the opposite order and tied pairs can differ from GEOS (the distance never does). Coordinates are origin-relative f32, so tiled copies shifted by thousands of units agree to about 4e-4. Not provided: multipoints.

GPUCoverageValidity

Checks polygons as one coverage for gaps, overlaps, crossings and unmatched shared edges, reported per ring segment.

::lens{scenario=gpu-spatial-analysis.coverage-validity rung=result caption="199 census tracts checked as one coverage with a 1 m gap width; shading counts invalid edges."}

Maximum: positions has at most getGPUSegmentIntersectionMaximumVertexCount(device) (4194304 on a default device: the 32-byte segment-table row per vertex must fit one storage binding) rows.

Polygon coverage validity (Shapely coverage_is_valid, coverage_invalid_edges(gap_width)). One BVH over all segments writes a per-segment flag word (unmatched, crossing, gap, overlap, uncertain bits), optional per-feature counts (featureInvalidCounts), a total (status.invalidCount) and isValid. The input is the four-level polygon layout (positions, featureOffsets, polygonOffsets, ringOffsets); segments of one feature are never compared. gapWidth is per-frame through getGPUCoverageValidityParameterValues. The output has one row per vertex and cannot overflow. Matched edges are skipped and flagged only when both interiors are on the same side, and a T-junction touch counts. The gap rule is the GEOS one (InvalidSegmentDetector::isNearlyParallel): each segment is projected onto the other and clipped to it, and the pair is a gap when both clipped projections are longer than gapWidth (strict) and their matching ends are within gapWidth of each other (inclusive). Staggered segments that run side by side over only part of their length, such as (0, 0)-(10, 0) beside (5, 0.0625)-(15, 0.0625) at gapWidth 0.125, are flagged (until 2026-11-11 they were not: the rule then asked for both ends of the shorter segment within gapWidth of the longer). The rule is evaluated in float32 relative to the start of the tested segment, so a distance or projected length equal to gapWidth to within float32 rounding can fall on either side. With Shapely 2.2.0 (GEOS 3.14.1) the invalid segment sets are equal on the 28 curated scenes (40 gap cases) and on the 222 cases of test/oracles/fixtures/coverage-validity-gaps.json (staggered rectangles, disjoint pairs and jittered triangulations, near the origin and at projected-metre coordinates). A matched edge lying inside a third polygon is not flagged (that polygon's own segments are). Ring winding is the exact turn at each ring's lexicographically smallest vertex, so a sliver whose float32 area is zero is still oriented. Every orientation sign is exact for the f32 vertices, including the winding number of the interior probe, which is the shared symbolic side probe (the point beside the exact midpoint of the segment, classified from the exact signs of the segment's endpoints), so a segment of a polygon thinner than one float32 step is not read as lying inside its neighbor. A pair with no decidable orientation (non-finite input) sets the uncertain bit. The operator is planar: spatialContext defaults to native planar coordinates and longitude-latitude throws (project first).
