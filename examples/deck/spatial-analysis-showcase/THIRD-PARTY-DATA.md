# Third-party data

The datasets under `public/data/` are third-party data. Their sources, licences, attribution text and
redistribution status are listed in the repository-level
[THIRD-PARTY-DATA.md](../../../THIRD-PARTY-DATA.md). Each `public/data/<id>/manifest.json` carries
the same `license` and `attribution` fields, and the build scripts and READMEs under `scripts/data/<id>/`
record how each extract was produced.
