Bounded facility allocation

Exhaustive p-center or capacitated p-median: scores every facility subset (up to 65536) in parallel.

operation
4
1.2

The chosen facilities (bright) and the demand each serves within its capacity.

Intersections follow TIGER/Line geometry. Bidirectional links and travel costs are modeled.

Reading the result

Best when the candidate set is small and you want the exact optimum, not a heuristic. Each GPU thread evaluates one subset against the cost matrix. Capacities are enforced for the capacitated variant.

Parameters

PropTypeDescription
operation*"p-center" | "capacitated-p-median"'p-center' or 'capacitated-p-median'.
facilityCount*numberNumber of candidate facilities.
selectCount*numberHow many to select.
costs*GraphDataView<"float32">Demand x facility cost matrix.
demand*GraphDataView<"float32">Demand per point.
capacities*GraphDataView<"float32">Capacity per facility.
output*{selectedFacilities, assignments}selectedFacilities, assignments, objective, status.

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
selectedFacilitiesGraphDataView<'uint32'>Best subset.
assignmentsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Facility per demand point.
objectiveGraphDataView<'float32'>Optimal objective value.
statusGraphDataView<'uint32'>Result status code.

Example

import {GPUBoundedFacilityAllocation} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-spatial-analysis';

graph.add(new GPUBoundedFacilityAllocation({
  operation: 'capacitated-p-median',
  facilityCount: 12,
  selectCount: 4,
  costs: costs.importToGraph(graph),
  demand: demand.importToGraph(graph),
  capacities: capacities.importToGraph(graph),
  output: {selectedFacilities, assignments, objective, status}
}));
