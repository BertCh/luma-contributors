Drive-time catchment

Snaps facilities and demand to the network, then bands demand by drive time to the nearest facility.

0.25
3
drawn

Demand points banded by drive time to the nearest facility.

Intersections follow TIGER/Line geometry. Bidirectional links and travel costs are modeled.

Data · San Francisco street network

Reading the result

Best for catchments such as demand within 5, 10 and 15 minutes of a clinic. It snaps points, runs service areas, bands the times and can build isochrone polygons. Band counts and value sums are written on the GPU.

  • Travel bands use the supplied edge costs; verify their units and convergence before calling them drive times.
