Expectation-based space-time scan

Finds the zones whose latest counts most exceed their baseline, with a Monte Carlo p-value.

12.0 h
4
drawn

GOES lightning, May 21–22 2024: expectation-based scan of the latest half hours against each zone’s previous 4 h; boxes and bright flashes are the most likely cluster. 4,096 sampled GLM flashes; p is not adjusted for repeated looks.

Reading the result

Prospective surveillance: every window ends at the latest bucket and counts are compared with a supplied baseline, so even a rise everywhere is found. Fast Subset Scan (Neill 2012) searches every subset of each neighborhood exactly.

Parameters

PropTypeDescription
positions*GraphDataView<"float32x2">Zone centroids.
cases*GraphDataView<"uint32">Observed counts, zone * timeBuckets + bucket.
baseline*GraphDataView<"float32">Expected counts in the same layout.
timeBuckets*numberTime buckets per zone.
maximumNeighbors*numberNeighborhood size of each center, at most 64.
maximumWindowLength*numberLongest window in buckets, at most 32.
parameters*GraphDataView<"uint32">getGPUExpectationBasedScanParameterValues: seed, replicates.
output*GPUExpectationBasedSpaceTimeScanOutputCluster, statistics and summary views.
and 1 more required: maximumReplicates

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
clusterZonesGraphDataView<"uint32">Zones of the most likely cluster.
clusterStatisticsGraphDataView<"float32">Score, observed, expected, p-value.
statisticsGraphDataView<"float32">Observed maximum score, then one per replicate.
summaryGraphDataView<"uint32">Found, members, window length, center, replicates.
zoneStatisticsGraphDataView<"float32">Optional best score centered at each zone.

Example

import {
  GPUExpectationBasedSpaceTimeScan,
  getGPUExpectationBasedScanParameterValues
} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-spatial-analysis';

// cases and baseline: zones x 16 buckets; baseline = moving average of earlier buckets
graph.add(new GPUExpectationBasedSpaceTimeScan({
  positions, cases, baseline, timeBuckets: 16,
  maximumNeighbors: 9, maximumWindowLength: 8, maximumReplicates: 199,
  parameters: graph.importBuffer(parameterBuffer, 'uint32'),
  output: {clusterZones, clusterStatistics, statistics, summary, zoneStatistics}
}));
parameterBuffer.write(
  getGPUExpectationBasedScanParameterValues({seed: 2, replicateCount: 99})
);
