Reading the result

Draws the biggest flows between zones from millions of trips. Origins and destinations map to zones, optionally filtered by a time window. The strongest pairs and zone in and out totals stay on the GPU.

Parameters

PropTypeDescription
zones*GPUFlowAggregationZonesZone lookup used to assign points.
originsGPUFloat32Positions | undefinedOrigin coordinates.
destinationsGPUFloat32Positions | undefinedDestination coordinates.
weightsGraphDataView<"float32"> | GraphVectorView<"float32">Weight per movement.
timeWindowGPUFlowAggregationTimeWindow | undefinedKeep movements inside a time range.
excludeSelfFlowsboolean | undefinedDrop flows whose ends share a zone.
pairCapacity*numberMaximum distinct zone pairs kept.
output*GPUFlowAggregationOutputTop-K flow list and per-zone totals.

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
flowOriginZoneIdsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Origin zone of each flow (optional).
flowDestinationZoneIdsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Destination zone of each flow (optional).
flowWeightsGraphDataView<'float32'>Summed weight per flow (optional).
zoneOutWeightsGraphDataView<'float32'>Total outflow per zone (optional).
zoneInWeightsGraphDataView<'float32'>Total inflow per zone (optional).
idsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Compact pair ids, one row per reported flow.
statusGPUCardinalityPort & GPUOverflowStatusPort & {pairOverflow?}Flow count and overflow flags.

Example

import {GPUFlowAggregation} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-network';

graph.add(new GPUFlowAggregation({
  zones,
  origins: origins.importToGraph(graph),
  destinations: destinations.importToGraph(graph),
  weights: passengers.importToGraph(graph),
  excludeSelfFlows: true,
  pairCapacity: 4096,
  output: {ids, status: {count, overflow}, flowWeights, zoneOutWeights, zoneInWeights}
}));
