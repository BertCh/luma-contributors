Network facility optimization

Exhaustive facility candidates evaluated in fixed-size GPU batches: capacitated p-median and backup coverage.

5
0.80
drawn

Candidate sites chosen for backup coverage (bright) among the demand locations.

Intersections follow TIGER/Line geometry. Bidirectional links and travel costs are modeled.

Reading the result

Best for exact siting problems with capacities or backup coverage thresholds. Candidates are enumerated in batches so larger sets stay bounded. Cap the work with maximumCandidates.

Parameters

PropTypeDescription
operation*"capacitated-p-median" | "backup-coverage" | "p-dispersion"'capacitated-p-median', 'backup-coverage' and similar.
facilityCount*numberNumber of candidate facilities.
selectCountnumber | undefinedHow many to select.
costs*GraphDataView<"float32">Demand x facility cost matrix.
demand*GraphDataView<"float32">Demand per point.
capacities*GraphDataView<"float32">Capacity per facility.
threshold*GraphDataView<"float32">Coverage cost threshold.
maximumCandidatesnumber | undefinedUpper bound on subsets examined.

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
selectedFacilitiesGraphDataView<'uint32'>Best subset.
assignmentsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Facility per demand point.
objectiveGraphDataView<'float32'>Objective value.
summaryGraphDataView<'uint32'>[valid, feasibleIncumbent, enumerationComplete, examinedCandidates].
status{invalidCount?, iterationCount?, converged?, iterationLimitReached?}Optional invalid-input and enumeration stopping diagnostics.

Example

import {GPUNetworkFacilityOptimization} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-network';

// summary: a uint32 view with 4 rows; status: optional named diagnostic views.
graph.add(new GPUNetworkFacilityOptimization({
  operation: 'backup-coverage',
  facilityCount: 8,
  selectCount: 5,
  costs: costs.importToGraph(graph),
  demand: demand.importToGraph(graph),
  capacities: capacities.importToGraph(graph),
  threshold: threshold.importToGraph(graph),
  output: {selectedFacilities, assignments, objective, summary, status}
}));
