Network structure

Exact strong components, bridges, articulation points and biconnected components on GPU-resident CSR.

output

Bridges (roads whose loss disconnects), articulation points, strong components.

Intersections follow TIGER/Line geometry. Bidirectional links and travel costs are modeled.

Reading the result

Finds the weak spots of a network: roads whose loss disconnects it, and islands that cannot reach the rest. Results are exact within the bounds of the algorithm. Choose which outputs to compute by supplying them.

Parameters

PropTypeDescription
topology*GPUNetworkAdjacencyCSR network, connectivity only.
reverseEdgeSlotsGraphDataView<"uint32"> | undefinedReverse slot of every CSR edge.
output*{strongComponents?}strongComponents, bridges, articulationPoints, biconnectedComponents.

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
strongComponentsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Strongly connected component label per node.
bridgesGraphDataView<'uint32'>Edges whose removal disconnects the graph.
articulationPointsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Nodes whose removal disconnects the graph.
biconnectedComponentsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Biconnected component label per edge.

Example

import {GPUNetworkStructure} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-network';

graph.add(new GPUNetworkStructure({
  topology,
  reverseEdgeSlots: reverseSlots.importToGraph(graph),
  output: {bridges, articulationPoints, strongComponents}
}));
