Reading the result

Use it to turn timestamps into the period axis of a cube. Fixed periods divide exact Int64 words by a width; calendar periods count whole steps from calendar columns. Times outside the periods are skipped and counted, never clamped.

Parameters

PropTypeDescription
temporalContext*GPUTemporalContextUnit and epoch of the Int64 row times.
periods*GPUPeriodIndexCalendarPeriods | GPUPeriodIndexFixedPeriodsCalendar steps or a fixed width, with origin and count.
timestampsGraphDataView<'uint32x2'>Int64 words per row, for fixed periods.
columnsGPUPeriodIndexCalendarColumnsCalendar columns, for calendar periods.
maskGraphDataView<'uint32'>Zero rows get no period.

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
periodsGraphDataView<'uint32'>Period per row, or a sentinel when out of range or masked.
statusGPUInvalidStatusPort | undefinedinvalidCount: valid times outside the periods.

Example

import {GPUPeriodIndex} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/dataframe-analysis';

// timestamps (uint32x2 Int64 words), periods, invalidCount: views on graph
graph.add(new GPUPeriodIndex({
  temporalContext: {unit: 'millisecond', epoch: 'unix', encoding: 'int64-words', axis: {kind: 'irregular'}},
  periods: {kind: 'fixed', width: 3_600_000n, origin: 1_716_292_800_000n, count: 18},
  timestamps,
  output: {periods, status: {invalidCount}}
}));
