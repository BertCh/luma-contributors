Spatial rate

Computes pooled neighborhood rates: the focus plus its neighbors, never an average of rates.

Spatially smoothed rates; islands are blank unless they keep their own rate.

Populations and event counts are modeled using tract poverty estimates.

Reading the result

Stabilizes small-area rates by borrowing strength from neighbors. It sums events and populations over the focus and its neighbors, then divides, as GeoDa does. Islands are undefined unless you choose own-rate.

Parameters

populations*GPUFloat32OrUint32Values-Positive population at risk per feature.
weights*GPUSpatialWeights | GPUChunkedSpatialWeights-Binary neighbor structure; numeric weights are ignored.
maskGraphDataView<"uint32"> | undefined-Nonzero rows count as focus and neighbor.
scalenumber | undefined1Compile-time rate multiplier, for example 100000.

Example

import {addSpatialRateRecipe} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-spatial-analysis';

// graph: GPUCommandGraph; weights are queen or KNN CSR from a weights contributor
const smoothed = addSpatialRateRecipe(graph, {
  events, populations, weights,
  scale: 100000,
  islandPolicy: 'own-rate'
});
const compiled = await graph.compileAsync();
compiled.encode(device.commandEncoder, {parameters: undefined});
// smoothed.outputs.spatialRates: events per 100,000 people
