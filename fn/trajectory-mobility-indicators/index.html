One day of twelve Bay vessel legs, scaled to leave room for the bars. Indicators made for people over weeks are here read on vessels. Bars: radius of gyration (blue) and real entropy (amber). Distances are approximate: the frame stretches x and y differently.

Reading the result

The scikit-mobility individual measures and trackintel home and tracking-quality rules, computed from staypoints and their locations, one thread per track. Meant for people over weeks; on vessels, home is the berth.

Parameters

PropTypeDefaultDescription
trackCount*number-Number of tracks, the row count of per-track outputs.
timestamps*GraphDataView<"float32"> | GraphDataView<"uint32x2">-Fix times: float32, or Int64 epoch-millisecond words.
staypoints*GPUMobilityStaypointColumns-Staypoint tracks, centroids, start rows, durations, locations, count.
triplegsobject | undefined-Tripleg table, needed for trackingQuality.
maximumEntropySequenceRowsnumber | undefined1024Cap on a track's staypoint sequence for realEntropy.
spatialContextGPUSpatialContext | undefined-Planar (default) or longitude/latitude great-circle.
and 1 more required: output

Returns

FieldTypeDescription
radiusOfGyrationGraphDataView<"float32"> | undefinedRMS distance of stays from their mean.
realEntropyGraphDataView<"float32"> | undefinedLempel-Ziv entropy of the location sequence.
homeLocationsGraphDataView<"uint32"> | undefinedLocation with the longest summed stay.
trackingQualityGraphDataView<"float32"> | undefinedTracked duration over extent.

Example

import {GPUMobilityIndicators} from '@luma-contributors/analysis/gpu-spatial-analysis';

// graph: GPUCommandGraph; staypoint columns from GPUTrajectoryStaypoints and Locations
graph.add(new GPUMobilityIndicators({
  trackCount,
  timestamps,
  staypoints: {tracks, centroids, startRows, durations, locationIds,
    status: {count, overflow: staypointOverflow, candidateOverflow: staypointCandidateOverflow}},
  output: {radiusOfGyration, realEntropy, randomEntropy, homeLocations, workLocations}
}));
const compiled = await graph.compileAsync();
compiled.encode(device.commandEncoder, {parameters: undefined});
