# Recipe inputs

Run `node examples/deck/spatial-analysis-showcase/scripts/data/extract-card-recipes.mjs` from the repository root to regenerate the four fixtures in `recipes/`. The script reads the checked-in showcase datasets and their manifests. The website ships only the selected rows and cells.

| Runs | Observation and transformation | Scenario controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot spot analysis, space-time hot spots, period comparison | 621 earthquake reports in the showcase ComCat extract between 34–44° E and 35–41° N in 2023. Epicenters are rounded to 0.001°, magnitude to 0.1 and times retained to the second. The February Türkiye–Syria sequence accounts for 461 reports. | Gi* neighborhood size and magnitude cutoff, monthly trend window, and first-quarter versus selected-quarter count differences. The comparison uses local display coordinates as its Quadbin index window. |
| Landscape metrics, patch proximity | Chicago Hyde Park area, ESA WorldCover v200 (2021), tile N39W090. The 578 × 432 native window is majority-reduced to 96 × 72 classes. The live lattice samples the central strip with square analysis cells across a roughly 4 km width. | Sampling grain and class generalization vary before labeling four-connected patches. Urban, water, trees, grass, bare and other classes are grouped according to the selected class count. |
| Change of support, dasymetric redistribution, exposure by zone, raster zonal distribution, rate cluster map, resistance connectivity, sparse resistance connectivity | Hyde Park built-up fraction from the same source. The showcase area-averaged the native built-class indicator onto a 64 × 64 equal-area grid at 62.5 m. The fixture stores the fraction in 8 bits. | Reporting zones and transfers are constructed. Population, hazard intensity, case counts and habitat conductance are illustrative functions of local built-up coverage. Walls, gaps and slider-induced variability are modeled. |
| Population support alignment, segregation counterfactual and decomposition | Twelve Hyde Park Census tracts and 29,559 estimated residents. The source stores 2018–2022 ACS total and non-Hispanic Black population estimates, linked by GEOID. The extractor retains the population pair and one boundary ring per tract. Earcut triangulates the rings, then a locally scaled longitude/latitude frame fits the drawing. | Absent/repeated IDs are deliberate join scenarios. Segregation uses the ACS estimate for the baseline, then changes focal share and its spatial concentration to construct comparison and counterfactual periods. |
| Regional raster change, surface preparation | Sentinel-2 L2A acquired 2021-07-13, 2021-09-21, 2023-07-13 and 2024-08-01 over the Dixie Fire area. The corresponding NDVI archive slices (7, 9, 12, 15) are center-cropped to 256 × 144 source cells. Each output cell averages valid observations within a 4 × 4 block. Zero represents unavailable data and decodes to NaN. | Regional change subtracts selected acquisitions within constructed reporting zones. Surface preparation adds adjustable holes and noise to the July 2021 scene. |
| Drainage network | Measured USGS 3DEP Grand Canyon elevation with square cell spacing in meters, shared with terrain runs. | Vertical exaggeration varies and the GPU recalculates accumulation and stream order. |
| Straight-line catchments, interpolation diagnostics | NOAA GHCN-Daily monitoring sites and gridded/interpolated observations from the shared Helene rainfall extract. Catchments select 14 stations in the Carolinas study window. | Facility count, cutoff distance, interpolation parameters and diagnostic scenarios. |
| Cell-flow topology | Chicago LEHD LODES8 2021 job counts, highest-volume home/work tract pairs from the shared commuting fixture. Tracts are assigned to the nearest vertex in the display lattice. | Pair cutoff and a modeled eastward weight. The default uses the observed counts. |
| Cluster and outline, points in polygons choropleth, zonal distribution | Shared earthquake observations supply the event positions. | Cluster extent and constructed reporting units support the aggregation and distribution exercises. |
| Excess risk, spatial rate, local variability, quantile LISA, spatial regression | Shared Chicago tract boundaries, poverty estimates and rook neighborhoods. | Rate numerators, sampling variability and selected islands are modeled. The regression uses observed poverty and age 65+ shares, scaled to the sample range. Its response uses a prescribed slope varying east to west and simulated spatially correlated errors. |
| Clock encounters, fleet dwell, fleet dwell zone events, zone events, track similarity/search, closest approach, trajectory encounters, Kalman, metrics, playhead, resample and restriction | The shared vessel fixtures preserve observed Bay Area AIS tracks. | The vessel source note describes playback timing, observation noise and modeled comparison tracks. |
