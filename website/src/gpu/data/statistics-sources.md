# Statistical demo data

Run `python3 website/scripts/data/prepare-statistics.py` from the repository root to refresh the three extracts below. The script uses the Python standard library and the checked-in showcase county data. The website loads the local snapshots.

| Snapshot | Observations and interpretation | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kilauea-events.json` | 350 earthquakes of magnitude 3.5 or greater during the Kīlauea eruption. The query covers 19.25–19.65°N and 155.4–154.8°W from May 1 through August 29, 2018 UTC. The excerpt keeps HVO locations only. Longitude and latitude are rounded to four decimals, and time is elapsed days since May 1 rounded to four decimals. Geographic distribution compares three successive 40-day windows. Knox and Mantel preserve all measured time-location pairs at their defaults. The observed-time slider permutes a selected share of origin times. | U.S. Geological Survey, Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, ANSS ComCat. U.S. government public-domain data. [Catalog service](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/) · [HVO](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/hvo) |
| `chicago-workplace-jobs.json` | Six annual total job counts for each of 156 Chicago tracts, 2018–2023. For each year, sum `C000` from Illinois WAC `S000_JT00` by the first eleven digits of the workplace block GEOID and retain the selected Chicago GEOIDs. Distribution dynamics and Markov runs use a common log(1 + jobs) scale over the six years. Counts describe jobs at workplaces. The upstream estimates use disclosure protection and modeling. | U.S. Census Bureau, Longitudinal Employer-Household Dynamics, LODES8, public-use U.S. government data. [Workplace Area Characteristics files](https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/lodes/LODES8/il/wac/) · [LEHD data](https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/) |
| `illinois-births.json` | 102 Illinois county centers, 2021–2023 resident births summed across estimate years, and estimated woman-years aged 15–44 over the same period. The scan compares counts to this population at risk. At contrast one it uses the published counts. The display shows observed window likelihood ratios. | U.S. Census Bureau Population Estimates Program, Vintage 2023. U.S. government public-domain data. [PEP](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest.html) · [Joined county inputs and source columns](../../../../examples/deck/spatial-analysis-showcase/scripts/data/us-county-births/README.md) |

## Inputs shared across runs

- Chicago regionalization partitions tracts using observed poverty and unemployment shares, rook adjacency and population thresholds measured in residents. Rate-smoothing runs take ACS counts of residents aged 65+ and total residents. Full contrast and unit population scale recover the source counts. Similar locations compares poverty, unemployment, age 65+ share and total population. Multiple testing adjusts 199-permutation local Moran pseudo p-values on the measured poverty shares. Boundary gap controls contract whole measured polygons by a specified fraction to model registration errors. [Tract sources](./area-sources.md)
- Areal interpolation transfers measured Chicago population totals or poverty shares into illustrative service districts, with incomplete coverage on the west. The pycnophylactic demo redistributes measured tract totals onto a grid. Both rasterize Census boundaries on the GPU. [Tract sources](./area-sources.md)
- Regression and GWR use measured tract poverty and age 65+ as scaled predictors. The response, spatial dependence, perturbations, regime splits and panel extensions are controlled simulations with known coefficients. Segregation uses illustrative group compositions and Census population totals. [Tract sources](./area-sources.md)
- The gravity-model scatterplot compares 205 published high-volume Chicago commute links among 24 selected tracts, using log-distance between tract internal points as its covariate. Its axes use log(1 + jobs). Rose statistics bins headings of resampled San Francisco Bay vessel legs, excluding stationary fixes and optionally discarding short movements. [Travel data and selection](./network-sources.md)
- Nested interpolation scores log-scaled rainfall at Carolina gauges on September 27, 2024. Spatially cross-validated OLS predicts that rainfall from September 26 readings and station location. Noise controls add a modeled perturbation, zero at the defaults. The point-pattern envelope compares sampled Japanese epicenters to simulated complete spatial randomness in the display window. That window includes the padding used to fit the map, so these curves are illustrative. [Station and earthquake observations](./point-sources.md)
- Emerging hot spots bins the Midwest GLM flash sample into twelve 90-minute windows and a 48-by-27 grid. The temporal-contrast control moves each cell toward its own time average. Lattice weights computes rook or queen neighbors over a reduced Grand Canyon elevation grid, masking cells below the selected elevation in meters. [Lightning source](./series-sources.md) · [Terrain source](./terrain-sources.md)
